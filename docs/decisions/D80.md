# D80: Subagent limits and UI scale in Settings, turn and step rails

Date: 2026-11-14. Area: crates/fast-harness-gui (Settings, chat transcript), crates/fast-harness-core (config, subagents).

## Decision

- `subagent.maxDepth` defaults to 1 (was 2): the top-level session can start subagents, they cannot start their own. 0 turns subagents off. `subagent.maxParallel` stays 4.
- Settings > Appearance gets a Subagents section (`ui/limits.slint`, `SubagentLimits`): max subagents at once (1 to 16) and max nesting depth (0 to 5). A change applies from the next `subagent` call and is saved with `config::save_section_setting` (a `store::update` of one key inside `subagent`, D55).
- The tool list stays frozen per session (D36): the top-level session always lists the `subagent` tool, so a Settings change never changes the tools, and `child_run::start` refuses a call when the session's depth is at or above the current `maxDepth` ("subagents are off at this depth"). With `maxDepth` 0 every call is refused that way.
- UI scale (`UiScaleSetting`): one slider and one number box, 50 % to 400 %, saved as `uiScale` (0.5 to 4). A drag previews live; the value is saved on release or on a number box edit. `limits::apply_scale` dispatches `WindowEvent::ScaleFactorChanged` and then `Resized` to the unchanged physical size, so the window keeps its size and the content scales. The Windows display scale stays ignored (D75): winit's own scale change never reaches Slint while `SLINT_SCALE_FACTOR` is set.
- Rails in the chat: an expanded step shows a faint vertical rail (`StepRail`, 2 px `Theme.dim` at alpha 0.32, 3 px on hover) left of its detail rows; a click on the rail folds the step. Each turn has the same rail from its prompt down, foldable too (expanded by default); a folded turn shows "N steps · first line of the last answer" (`src/turn_rows.rs`). Steps sit one indent (20 px) inside their turn, step details one more. Approvals and questions waiting for the user never fold. Folded rows stay in the model and build no content (each part's `if` also tests `!msg.turn-hidden`), so they are zero high.

- The turn navigator's card (D77) is as tall as its texts (`height: lay.preferred-height`, was 98 px), the preview at most three lines; it previews the turn's final reply: the last assistant text with words, not the first.

## Fixed 2026-11-14 (gui-rail-fixes)

- `rails-folded` showed turn 2 open: the shot passed `turn_nav::Turn::row` (the user row) to `Mirror::toggle_turn`, whose turns start after it, so it closed a row that heads no turn. `toggle_turn` now takes the user row too. The fold itself reached the window (`src/rails_view_tests.rs`).
- The step rail broke at every row: each row's segment had rounded 1 px ends, so two dim pixels showed at each join. Segments are square; the hover lights the whole rail (`Rails` global in `ui/stepline.slint`).
- Turn 1's answer did carry its rail (pixel check of the shot): the faint rail was lost in a scaled-down view.

## Fixed 2026-11-14 (gui-fold-blank)

- Rows after a folded turn drew blank: the rows had `visible: false` and `max-height: 0`, but a row's min height (its wrapped text) beats max-height, so every hidden row kept its height (instrumented: row 9 of turn 2, turn-hidden, stayed 100 px; the turn-line row 108 px). The parts now sit under `if root.shown && ...` (components, safe from slint#13655): the hidden rows are 0 px, the turn line 40 px. The navigator's target row, when the list's estimate leaves it unlaid (`go(0)` landed in turn 1's answer), is sought half a view at a time (`seek` in `ui/transcript.slint`) until its pin takes over. Tests: `src/fold_view_tests.rs` (turn and step fold and unfold at top, middle, bottom; navigator ticks after a fold), `rails_view_tests::the_rows_after_a_turn_folded_while_scrolled_draw_at_once` no longer ignored.
