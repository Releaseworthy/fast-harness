# D89 Shell output: binary as a whole, an even cut, hints

Owner, 2026-11-15:

1. A shell printing a binary file still leaked short garbage lines between the `[binary output, N bytes omitted]` notes, and the count was wrong (a 100 KB file reported as ~190 KB).
2. A cut shell output kept 7 head lines and 20 tail lines; build errors sit at the start of the output.
3. `gci -Recurse` went into folders `.gitignore` leaves out, `Get-Content` read files, and every line repeated the absolute working folder.

## Decision

1. `tools/clean.rs` `clean_output`: an output whose characters are more than a tenth bad (control characters other than whitespace and ESC, NUL, U+FFFD), with at least 16 of them, is binary as a whole and becomes the one line `[binary output, N bytes]`. N is the size before the lossy decode: pwsh writes each byte it could not decode as U+FFFD (3 bytes in UTF-8), so each of those counts as one byte; raw bytes (`cmd /c type`) count as they are. Text with a few odd characters or colour codes stays text, and binary runs inside text still become `[binary output, N bytes omitted]`.
2. Why 7 and 20: no commit set those numbers. Since Phase 12 (8ef3fd9) `head_tail` gave the head a quarter of the byte room ("errors are at the end"); D84 (90cef47, bc52ec5) added the 20/30 line caps and the shared step budget (`context.stepOutputBytes`, at least 2 KB a call). With a 2 KB share and lines of ~70 bytes, a quarter is ~7 lines while the tail fits 20. The quarter was intentional for test failures at the end; the 7 lines were its side effect. Now the head gets half the room and the tail the rest plus what the head left unused, at most 25 lines each (`tools/budget.rs` `SHELL_HEAD_LINES`, `SHELL_TAIL_LINES`); more than 50 lines are still cut and the whole output still spills to `<session>.out`.
3. `tools/shell_hints.rs` adds one line each, before `[exit code: N]`, only when it applies; the command is never blocked:
   - a recursive listing (`Get-ChildItem`, `gci`, `ls`, `dir` with `-Recurse` or a prefix, `-Depth`, `dir /s`) whose output names a path inside a folder some `.gitignore` from the repository root down leaves out (`tools/shell_ignored.rs`): names up to three such folders and points at `glob`;
   - `Get-Content`, `gc`, `cat` or `type` with a path (not `-Wait`): points at `read`;
   - the working folder plus a separator in front of paths on at least 3 lines: left out everywhere, with one note naming it.
