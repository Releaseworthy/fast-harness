# D84: Tighter cuts and a step budget

Owner, 2026-11-14: four places still let too much enter the context.

1. A long line in `read` was cut at 2000 characters while `grep` cuts at 300.
2. A long shell output kept a quarter of the 8 KB budget as head and the rest as tail: with short lines that is a few hundred lines, while the whole output is already in the spill file.
3. A `glob` or `grep` outside the working directory repeated the long absolute start of every path (100 glob paths ~2k tokens of shared prefix).
4. One step with several broad calls (read + grep + glob) could add every call's full budget at once (~10.6k tokens on his log).

## Decision

1. `read` cuts a line at 800 characters (`tools/read.rs` `MAX_LINE_CHARS`), inside the 500 to 1000 he named; the description says so.
2. `shell` and `job_output` keep at most 40 lines from the start and 60 from the end (`tools/budget.rs` `SHELL_HEAD_LINES`, `SHELL_TAIL_LINES`), still inside the byte room. An output of more than 100 lines is cut and spilled even under the byte budget. The marker names the lines and bytes left out: `[... N lines, B bytes cut ...]`.
3. When `path` names a folder other than the working directory, `glob` and `grep` show found paths relative to it, after one first line `[paths relative to <folder>/]` (`tools/args.rs` `search_base`, `BASE_MARK`). The tool cards skip that line.
4. `context.stepOutputBytes` (default 16384, 0 turns it off): the calls of one step share it. Each of the `n` sharing calls gets `min(own budget, max(stepOutputBytes / n, 2048))`, decided before the calls run (`config/context.rs` `step_share`, `agent/tool_run.rs`). One or two calls keep their 8 KB. `find_tools` (not budgeted) and subagent answers (`subagent.outputBytes`) do not share. A call cut by its share spills its whole text as before.

Nothing already sent changes (D83): every cut happens before the first send.
