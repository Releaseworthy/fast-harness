# D76 Settings and About are dialogs; Subagents and Jobs are tabs

Status: done (2026-11-14).

## Decision

The owner's request: Settings and About open as their own popups, not as tabs next to Chat and Trace, and the subagents and background jobs triggers sit where Chat and Trace are.

- Settings and About are modal dialogs over the whole window (`ui/dialog.slint` `Dialogs`, `ModalDialog`): a dimmed backdrop (`Theme.shadow` at 0.6), a centred card sized to its content and at most 80% of the window, a title with a close button, its own scroll. Escape, the close button or a click on the backdrop closes it; nothing under the backdrop takes a click or a wheel. The focus moves into the dialog when it opens and back to the composer when it closes (not while a subagent's read-only session shows). The content is unchanged (`ui/pages.slint` `SettingsBody`: Appearance with chat detail and renderer, model, prompt, configuration, UI files; `AboutBody`).
- `DialogUi.open` ("", "settings" or "about") says which is open. `open-page("settings")` and `open-page("about")` (the title bar buttons, the keys.json `open_settings` action) reach `src/dialogs.rs` `open` and leave the shown page as it is; opening a panel from the configuration section closes the dialog. There is no Settings or About page or tab any more; Accounts and panels keep their closable third tab.
- The subagents (D74) and background jobs (D72) triggers are tabs right after Trace (`ui/subagents.slint` `SubagentsTab`, `ui/jobs.slint` `JobsTab`, both `ui/pill.slint` `PopTab`): "Subagents 3", "Jobs 2", the count in a badge with a dot while one runs (a shape, not colour alone), the accessible label "Subagents: 3, 2 running". Each tab shows only while the shown session has subagents or jobs. A click opens the same popover as before, now under its tab (`PopupWindow` at the tab, at most 560px wide and the room down to the status bar), and the chat stays visible under it. The pill row above the composer is gone; the read-only notice of a child session stays in the composer's place.

## Checks

- `src/tabbar_tests.rs` (software window, whole `MainWindow`): no Subagents tab without subagents, the tab opens the popover, a row click opens the child read-only, Back returns; no Jobs tab without jobs, the tab opens the popover, Escape closes it; the Settings and About title bar buttons open dialogs (the page stays "chat"), Escape closes each, a backdrop click closes About. The open-child and back callbacks are wired by the test as the view does: the app's `subagents_view::open_child` path is covered by its own tests, not here.
- `src/shots_tabbar.rs` (`tabbar_shots`, ignored): both tabs, each popover, both dialogs, a child session read-only.
