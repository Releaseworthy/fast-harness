# D19: GUI frame

Owner, 2026-11-11: a custom title bar like DSH's. Frameless window (`no-frame`, 6 px resize border, winit) with a 40 px title bar: sidebar toggle, Settings, About, minimize, maximize or restore, close (the account usage pill was removed 2026-11-12, D27). Slint's `WindowMoveArea` drags the window; a double click on the empty bar maximizes; every window state change goes through `window_state.rs`. The sidebar holds the logo, New session, the sessions and, at the bottom, only an Accounts entry with a person icon. Chat and Trace are tabs of the session; Settings, About and Accounts open as a third, closable tab.
