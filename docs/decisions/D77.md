# D77: Turn navigator and wider chat margins

Date: 2026-11-14. Area: crates/fast-harness-gui (chat transcript).

## Decision

- The chat column gets symmetric side margins from `Columns.chat(width)` (`ui/parts.slint`): 48 px at normal widths, shrinking to 16 px on narrow views before the content shrinks; the column still stops at `Theme.column-width`. The composer, banner and rows under the transcript use the same column.
- A turn navigator (`ui/turnnav.slint`, `TurnNav`, global `TurnsUi`) sits in the right gutter between the column and the scroll bar, vertically centred (60 px clear at top and bottom for Jump to latest). One tick per user turn; the turn at the top of the view has the longer, darker tick. Tick spacing is at most 12 px and shrinks to fit; more than 60 turns are sampled evenly (first and last kept). Hidden below two turns or when the gutter is under 40 px.
- Hovering (or focusing) a tick shows a Card to its left: the prompt's first line in bold (elided) and the start of the first reply text in dim text (three lines). Click, Enter or Space scrolls to the turn; each tick is an accessible button "Go to turn N: <prompt>".
- The texts come from Rust (`src/turn_nav.rs`) after each transcript sync and are handed to the window only when they changed; a reply is read only up to 4x the preview length per delta. No timers.
- Scrolling to a row: the ListView lays out only the rows in view, so `go-row` jumps to the row's share of the height, then the target row pins `content-y` to its own `y` from `init`, `changed pinned` and `changed y` until a user scroll (wheel or bar) or Jump to latest. While a target is pinned the current tick follows the target; otherwise the row whose `at-top` turns true sets it (`changed` handlers can miss a quick true-false-true while the list settles).
