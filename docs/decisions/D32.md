# D32: Accounts as provider cards

**Moved to branch `antigravity` (D53), not on main.** Owner, 2026-11-12: providers split, as cards. The Accounts page opens on a grid of provider cards (Claude, Google Antigravity): a monochrome provider logo tinted from the theme, the name, the account count and a status in words (the active account, "Needs setup" for Google without an OAuth client, the lowest quota left). A card opens that provider's page with a back button: its accounts and actions (Claude: sign in, add an API key; Google: sign in, and an OAuth client form that writes `google-client.json` as D25, the secret masked and never shown again). TUI: the Accounts panel lists the providers first; Enter opens one, Esc goes back.
